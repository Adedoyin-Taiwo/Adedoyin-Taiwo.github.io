01 / About

Research that takes people’s real lives seriously.

I am Adedoyin Taiwo, a quantitative researcher, educator, and founder. My work begins with a simple conviction: health is lived in changing contexts, not in isolated snapshots.

I bring together dynamical systems theory, intensive longitudinal data, Bayesian and multilevel modelling, and predictive methods to understand regulation, stress, and health behaviour over time.
