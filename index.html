01 / About

Research that takes people’s real lives seriously.

I am Adedoyin Taiwo, a health psychology researcher, data educator, and founder. My work begins with a simple conviction: health is lived in changing contexts, not in isolated snapshots.

I bring together theory, rigorous methods, and public-facing communication to understand how people regulate health behaviours, respond to stress, and sustain wellbeing over time.
